# Living society update

The September 8 request makes individual people the primary targets for divine intervention. This contract supersedes the earlier agent ownership notes for this integrated update.

The release must provide selectable profiles with a visual stat chart, age and life stage, gender, adult orientation, job, character, illness, wealth, spouse, parents, children, personal history, and direct actions. Relationships refer to real persistent citizen identities. Adults form compatible relationships; children have no declared adult orientation or marriage actions. Gender and orientation do not determine morality, competence, crime, or worth.

Families, aging, illness, bereavement, work, education, honorable deeds, theft, murder, investigations, imprisonment, corruption, laws, and rebellion must change saved state. Political intervention acts through the selected ruler or council member. Healing a sick relative can fulfill a specific named adult's promise, with benevolent or harmful consequences. A tyrant can provoke organized opposition, succession, secession, and an actual civil war. Outcomes depend on circumstances and character; no claim to simulate every possible human behavior.

Keep map powers for terrain and broad natural events. Healing, curse, possession, purification, bribery, and personal bargains require an identified person and a preview explaining who is affected. Clicking a pictured person opens their profile; a compact profile with tabs keeps the world usable on a phone.

Preserve old saves by deterministic migration. Preserve the Android signing identity and music-loop crash fix. Validate relationship references, mortality, birth parentage, individual targeting, promises, laws and civil wars, save continuation, rejection without mutation, phone layouts, and real Android startup before publishing the next Android release.
