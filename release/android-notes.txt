The Gods 1.7.1 — Cast & See

Power selection responds immediately during fast-forward. Click or touch the map to cast an area power. While a year is busy, each cast keeps the power, location and radius you chose; a marker confirms it is queued. Observe or Escape cancels pending casts.

Healing, curses and possession still affect a specific person: select the power, then touch a person or community to choose the target. Personal results focus the camera on that person, show an animated named marker and actual stat/condition changes, and keep time paused for inspection.

Impact effects remain visible for real seconds at every speed. Meteor strikes, rain, storms and earthquakes also work over water where appropriate. Fast-forward retains time spent processing a year; high speeds remain limited by the device's simulation capacity.

Single-player sandbox; powers remain free. Install over the existing app to retain saves. Version 1.7.1, Android code 10701. Desktop mouse and host touch fixtures tested; no new physical-phone review.
